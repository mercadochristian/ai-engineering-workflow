# AI use and evidence

## Who did what

Christian selected the practices to retain, corrected the workflow when its
results were untrustworthy, set privacy and publication boundaries, and requested
this portfolio. An AI assistant helped inspect workflow references and historical
summaries, draft and organize these documents, and write the synthetic example and
checks. This repository is AI-assisted work; its prose and example are not claimed
to be unaided authorship.

In the underlying work, AI assisted with investigation, implementation, testing,
and review. Christian owned the scope, constraints, acceptance decisions, and
authorization. Each case study explains the specific contribution and judgment
involved without pretending that AI output was automatically correct.

## Three kinds of material

| Material | What readers can conclude |
| --- | --- |
| Historical case studies | My account of work and lessons, supported during preparation by private records |
| Templates and instructions | The workflow I intend to apply; instructions alone do not prove compliance |
| Synthetic example and tests | Behavior readers can reproduce in this repository |

Private records were consulted to keep the retrospective accurate. They are not
included or linked here. Readers cannot independently verify the original project
or its test runs through this repository. Internal counts, identifiers, and
product details have been omitted. The examples preserve the engineering lesson,
not the original implementation.

## Claims I am not making

- Measured improvements in delivery time, cost, token use, or defect rate.
- That a simulated review is an independent behavioral benchmark.
- That local checks prove live integration or production deployment success.
- That this repository demonstrates training models or conducting AI research.
- That all AI-generated work was correct on the first attempt.

## How I would measure improvement

For a future evaluation, record task type and complexity, starting revision,
tools and model configuration, elapsed effort, review findings, rework, and
acceptance results. Compare similar tasks under documented conditions and retain
failed outcomes. Describe sample size and confounders before drawing conclusions.

That evaluation is planned. It has not been performed for this portfolio.
