# Task brief

Use the sections that help the task. A small change does not need a separate file.

## Outcome

What should someone be able to do, and under what conditions?

## Scope and constraints

Affected behavior and callers, relevant instructions, privacy limits, existing
dependencies, and unrelated work to preserve.

## Ownership and authority

Who owns which files or decisions? What actions are authorized? Which external
effects, publication steps, or destructive actions require separate authorization?

## Acceptance

- Verify that the expected outcome occurs under an explicit success setup.
- Verify that the relevant denial or restriction still applies.
- Verify recovery when the identified failure condition occurs, if applicable.

## Verification

Safe target, fixture setup, relevant existing checks, and the observable evidence
needed for each boundary. Record an unavailable method as blocked or not run.

## Handoff

Changed paths, checks and results, unresolved questions, and the next decision.
